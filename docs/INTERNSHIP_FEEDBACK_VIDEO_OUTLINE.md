# Internship Experience and Feedback Video Outline

Use only facts you can personally verify. Replace bracketed placeholders before recording.

1. **Introduction:** “I am [name], and I completed my internship at [organization] from [dates].”
2. **Role and context:** “My role was [role]. My assigned area was [team or project area].”
3. **Work completed:** Describe [specific task], [specific deliverable], and [specific tool or method].
4. **Learning:** Explain one technical skill and one professional skill you developed, with a concrete example.
5. **Support and feedback:** Describe [mentor or team support] only if accurate.
6. **Challenge and response:** State one real challenge, what you tried, and the observed outcome.
7. **Feedback:** Offer constructive, factual feedback about onboarding, documentation, tools, communication, or training.
8. **Closing:** Thank [organization or people] only if you wish, then state how the experience informs your next step.
